## Final sweep (September 17, 2026)

Baseline for this sweep: `fix/reliability-remediation` with the CC-09 cancellation contract, and `feat/mousepad-verified-find-replace` at `8d94fcc`. Every plan-listed sub-requirement above falls into one of three dispositions; nothing is left unclassified. The full list with owners is in [`FOLLOWUPS.md`](FOLLOWUPS.md).

**Implemented and proven in a bounded slice** (retained under `cc01/`–`cc09/`): every "established" column entry above, plus — since the previous baseline — the CC-08 subscription-initialization repair, the CC-09 native event-to-storage latency measurement, and the CC-09 cancellation ownership/acknowledgement contract with native measurement.

**Human-only blockers** (cannot be advanced without a person):
- Human approval of the Mousepad batches (32-step fetch-retry and original 24-step, both GREEN on all three trials).
- Semantic Open: the native accessibility-bridge rebuild needs `libxml2-dev` (and root to install it); the peer-routing workaround was tried, failed readback in all three trials and was reverted.
- Gemini video inspection credential: `GOOGLE_API_KEY` is absent on this host; the original-24 visual review used frame-sampled Anthropic inspection instead, which is disclosed in that proof.
- Live human-takeover / revoke-resume (CC-05) and IME/autocomplete on a real user desk (CC-02) require a person at a physical desktop.

**Autonomous follow-ups, not yet done** (no human needed, but outside the bounded slices above): a live-induced degraded-ancestry case (CC-08; root-level health signal done in `cc08/health/`, reparenting in `cc08/reparent/`); normalized crop provenance and freshness (CC-07); live foreground/occlusion characterization (CC-01). These are recorded, not claimed. The stalled-consumer policy (CC-09) is decided and proven: ADR-0106, `cc09/stalled-consumer/`. Immediate unsubscribe cleanup under the real framework (CC-04) is done: `cc04/framework/`, ADR-0099 addendum.

## Separate Mousepad acceptance and blockers

The 32-step model-assisted visual batch and independent public-pointer reopening are separate bounded proofs. Original 24-step unpooled model acceptance is GREEN on t1–t3 at `8d94fcc` (instruction repair: step budget spent on unrequested readbacks/menus, not checkbox toggling); human approval pending. Semantic Open's rejected peer-routing experiment is retained on the Mousepad branch in `semantic-open/`; all three attempts failed later readback, all source bytes were unchanged, and the patch was reverted. A local native bridge build stopped at missing `libxml-2.0` development metadata. No system library replacement was made. Do not retry that route indefinitely or relabel it GREEN.

Section 13's packaged specialist, consumer adapters and skills/learning are explicitly future feature work, not CC-10 or additional numbered remediation. Sections 14's integration, fresh regression/gate/mutation receipts, independent review and honest public evidence remain shipping gates. This audit does not substitute for those gates.
